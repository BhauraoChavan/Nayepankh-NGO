About Us

NayePankh Foundation is a student-led NGO dedicated to education support, skill development, youth empowerment, social awareness, and community service.


We help students gain practical knowledge, confidence, leadership skills, and opportunities through internships, workshops, and campaigns.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide equal opportunities to students, empower youth through skill development, support communities, and create positive social change through awareness, education, and volunteer-driven initiatives.

NayePankh Foundation is a UP Govt, 80G & 12A Registered NGO. We are also one of the Biggest Student led Organization, working towards Uplifting Of Underprivileged. We welcome you to our NayePankh's Family 💙
